Install Stock Pilot

A phone web app you add to your home screen, plus a Windows setup program.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open your Stock Pilot address in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (iOS 26: tap ⋯ then Share) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open Stock Pilot from the home screen and sign in.

Works today as a home-screen shortcut. Full-screen app mode (no address bar, own icon + offline screen) mock up

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open your Stock Pilot address in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Stock Pilot from the home screen.

Welcome to Stock Pilot Setup mock up

This wizard will install Stock Pilot on your computer, add Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts, and walk you through connecting your broker.

CancelNext ›
  1. Double-click Stock-Pilot-Setup.exe. It isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info → Run anyway.
  2. Follow the wizard: I agree → your name → pick your broker → sign in to your broker → pick your account → trading preferences → finish. See each step mock up
  3. Remove it any time from Settings → Apps → Stock Pilot.

What you need

  • A brokerage account. Charles Schwab works today; other brokers mock up
  • Windows 10 or 11 for the desktop version mock up
  • An iPhone or Android phone for the phone app.
