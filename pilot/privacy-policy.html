Summary

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or renting of your data.
  • Your broker sign-in happens on your broker's own site; Stock Pilot never sees or stores your broker password.
  • Trading data stays between your device, the Stock Pilot service and your broker.

What we collect

  • Setup details: your name, chosen broker and trading preferences (amount per buy, buy list, sell rules).
  • Broker access token: the authorization token your broker issues after you sign in on its site, stored encrypted and used only to read balances/positions and place the orders you allowed.
  • Account and trade data: masked account number, balances, positions, orders, fills and P&L, read from your broker to run the app.
  • App health logs: times, errors and system checks, used to keep trading working. No device advertising IDs.
  • Support and donations: anything you send in a problem report; donations happen on Venmo or PayPal, and we never see your card or bank details.

How we use it

  • To run Stock Pilot: show your account, place and manage the trades you authorized, run trailing stops and sell-alls.
  • To keep it secure and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations. Never for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

  • Your broker: orders and account requests you authorized.
  • Service providers that host or run the app (for example Cloudflare and GitHub) under their own security and privacy terms.
  • Legal: if required by law, subpoena or to protect rights and safety.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Storage, security and retention

Tokens and settings are stored encrypted. Data is kept while your account is active and deleted within [30] days after you disconnect or ask us to delete it, except records we must keep by law. No system is 100% secure. Tell us right away at [CONTACT EMAIL] if you think your access was misused.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn automated trading off, disconnect your broker (also revoke access at your broker), or uninstall at any time.
  • Ask to access, correct, export or delete your data at [CONTACT EMAIL].
  • California, EU/UK and other residents may have extra rights under CCPA/CPRA, GDPR and similar laws; we honor them as required.

Children

Stock Pilot is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

These pages

The Stock Pilot web pages are static. They use no cookies or analytics; the setup preview saves and sends nothing. GitHub Pages may keep standard server logs.

Changes

We will update the "Last updated" date and show a notice in the app for material changes.
