Support

Help for Stock Pilot. Most answers are in the app under System.

Quick help

Where do I see if everything is running?

Open System. Systems Check shows each part in green, yellow or red, and the red badge on System means something needs a look. Tap an alert to acknowledge it.

When does Stock Pilot sell?

Every trade has a trailing stop. Day trades are sold by the 2:30 PM Central sell-all, with a 2:45 PM backup. Long-term lots are never sold by the sell-all.

How do I change how much each buy spends?

Open Buy List. Changes reach the trading computer in about a minute. There is no default amount: you set it.

What does "Van's List" mean?

The names Van manages directly. They trade alongside the Buy List picks.

Numbers look old or blank

Tap the refresh arrow at the top of the tab. Balances come from your broker's last scheduled read (about once a minute in market hours).
