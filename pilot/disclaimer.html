Trading stocks, especially day trading, carries a high risk of loss. You can lose some or all of the money you trade, and losses can happen very fast. Only trade money you can afford to lose.

Automated-trading risk

When you allow it, Stock Pilot places orders automatically, without asking you each time. Automated orders can be triggered by software bugs, bad or delayed market data, wrong settings or unusual market conditions. Trailing stops and market orders can fill at prices far from what you expect (slippage, gaps, halts). Orders already sent may fill even after you turn Stock Pilot off.

Broker, API and outage risk

Stock Pilot depends on your broker's API, market-data feeds, internet, hosting and your device. Any of them can be slow, wrong, rate-limited or down, which can cause missed, late, duplicate or rejected orders, or stops and sell-alls that do not run. We are not responsible for these failures.
