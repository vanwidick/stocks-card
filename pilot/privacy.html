About Stock Pilot©

A personal trading co-pilot. Design by Van.

What it does

  • Shows your Buy List, open trades, P&L and system health on your phone.
  • Places buys on your broker account and protects every trade with a trailing stop.
  • Sells day trades by 2:30 PM Central, with a 2:45 PM backup.

Privacy

  • Your broker sign-in happens on your broker's own site. Stock Pilot never stores your broker password.
  • The app shows masked account numbers only; no passwords, codes or tokens are shown.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • These support pages are static and collect nothing.

Disclosures

Stock Pilot is not a broker or financial adviser and gives no investment advice. Trading involves risk, including loss of money. You are responsible for all trades in your account.
